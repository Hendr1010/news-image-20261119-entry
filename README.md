# The World in One Day — Thursday 19 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of
Thursday 19 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image.

## The day in one page

- **VICE CITY OPENS** — Grand Theft Auto VI rolls out worldwide; nearly
  1 in 5 US workers plan time off. Consoles run at 30 fps; no 60 fps mode
  at launch.
- **THREE POWERS, ONE TABLE** — Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen in
  the first three-way summit; nuclear arms, Ukraine and Iran on the agenda.
- **GULF ON EDGE** — Iran and the US trade strikes across the Gulf; a
  tanker is hit off Hormuz.
- **VOYAGER 1: ONE LIGHT-DAY OUT** — NASA's probe is believed to have
  crossed a light-day from Earth, the farthest human object.
- **€45BN FOR UKRAINE** — the EU proposes a €45 billion loan, to be repaid
  after Russia pays reparations.
- **AID FOR FLOODED GAZA** — Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK jointly
  call on Israel to open humanitarian access.
- **ALSO TODAY** — France's 4th weekend of cost-of-living protests,
  Cyclone Akvan nears India, Cambridge word of 2026: "Human", Eisenkot
  tapped to form Israel's next government, Canada rethinks China ties.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, Pillow only) draws the whole front page with
vector shapes and text — no network, no external images, no randomness.
It writes the image to `/output/entry.png`. Running it twice produces
byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
